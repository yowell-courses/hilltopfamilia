Narrative

Line-item justification

A. Personnel — $9,792.00

  • Lead bilingual reporter/producer: oversees story development, Spanish scripts, fact-checking and WhatsApp coordination. $13 × 10 hrs/week × 34 weeks = $4,420.
  • Austin Vida civic reporting intern: extends coverage into Austin’s Latino community: civic affairs, housing, healthcare and education. $13 × 10 hrs/week × 34 weeks = $4,420.
  • Multimedia production assistant: video assembly, teleprompter operation, filming and caption quality assurance. $11 × 2.5 hrs/week × 34 weeks = $952.

Faculty advisement is contributed by existing Hilltop Views staff at no cost.

B. Fringe benefits — $749.09

FICA payroll taxes at 7.65% of student earnings ($9,792.00 × 0.0765).

C. Equipment — $360.00

A NEEWER X11 mobile teleprompter rig with integrated monitor ($220) lets presenters keep direct eye contact without AI gaze correction. A NEEWER TP72A fluid head tripod ($140) stabilizes the rig in the studio and in the field.

Existing camera, lenses and laptops valued at $1,400 are contributed at no cost.

D. Technology — $250.00

Annual domain and edge hosting for hilltopfamilia.org, including the optional drafting assistant ($100). Video editing and Spanish captioning software ($150).

E. Outreach and training — $450.00

Reporter onboarding materials on ethical Spanish-language reporting ($150). Print cards and WhatsApp invite codes for orientation, Family Weekend and First-Gen/HSA events ($300).

F–G. Contractual and indirect — $0.00

The Austin Vida collaboration is funded through the internship. Hilltop Views waives overhead, directing 100% of funds to the project.
