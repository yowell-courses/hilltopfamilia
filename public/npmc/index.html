Hilltop Familia

A Spanish-language campus and community news channel for the families of St. Edward’s University students.

Approximately 1,000 current students come from Spanish-speaking households.

View the MVP and prototype Resource requirements

Pitch story

Why Hilltop Familia exists

Context

Spanish-speaking parents want to stay connected to what is happening on campus and in Austin so they can support their students’ education. Right now, they are locked out because no information is available in their language.

Challenge

Families ask their children to translate, piece information together from community networks, or search for a staff member who can help. This does not work: the university provides no Spanish-language information, and local media does not focus on this community.

Solution

Hilltop Familia connects Spanish-speaking families directly to university information in their language. Parents, siblings, grandparents and the wider household can stay informed and help guide their student’s education and career.

Problem statement

An unmet information need

Spanish-speaking families of current students want campus and Austin community news so they can provide informed support as their students navigate their education and career. This need goes unmet because the university provides no Spanish-language information, and local Spanish media does not focus on this demographic.

OKRs

Objective and key results

Objective

Establish a dedicated Spanish-language communication channel to bridge the information gap and connect Spanish-speaking families to the university experience.

Delivery

Launch a pilot Spanish-language platform and publish 6 pieces of targeted campus and Austin content by the end of the quarter.

Adoption

Acquire at least 200 active subscribers or group members from the target audience of Spanish-speaking parents and families by month six of launch.

Engagement

Achieve an average open/read rate of 25% on published content.

Feedback

Collect survey feedback from 50 Spanish-speaking families to establish a baseline “university connection” score to improve upon next quarter.

Newsroom mission: Hilltop Views provides St. Edward’s University and the Central Texas community with an independent and reliable news source that covers events and issues and illuminates the student experience.

Lean canvas

Lean canvas

Designed for:Hilltop Views, St. Edward’s University
Designed by:Curt Yowell
Date:November 2026
Version:2.0

Problem ●

Spanish-speaking families of current students want campus and Austin community news so they can provide informed support as their students navigate their education and career.

The university provides no Spanish-language information, and local Spanish media does not focus on this demographic.

Existing alternatives

  • Students translating ad hoc
  • Information pieced together from community networks
  • Seeking help from specific program staff (for example, the CAMP director)

Solution ◆

A dedicated Spanish-language communication channel that bridges the information gap and connects Spanish-speaking families to the university experience.

Key Metrics ▲

  • Delivery: pilot platform live; 6 pieces of content published by the end of the quarter
  • Adoption: 200 active subscribers by month six
  • Engagement: 25% average open/read rate
  • Feedback: 50-family baseline “university connection” survey

Unique Value Proposition ★

Spanish-language news that helps families feel connected to, and support, their students and the St. Edward’s community, by removing language barriers with clear communication and quality journalism.

High-level concept

Campus news for Spanish-speaking families, in Spanish.

Unfair Advantage ★

  • Intimate knowledge of the target audience
  • Human translation

Channels ➤

  • WhatsApp
  • Website
  • Format experiments: video and text

Customer Segments ●

  • Parents
  • Siblings
  • Grandparents
  • Extended family
  • Students
  • Austin Spanish-speaking community

Early adopters

Spanish-speaking parents of current students, expected to be the most active users (about 1,000 families).
